# ALPHAODDS — AGENT 25 — ALPHABREADTH / MULTI-MARKET OPPORTUNITY EXPANSION — v1.0

## Mission
Expand AlphaOdds from a single selective first-touchdown system into multiple independent research lanes so a normal NFL week can produce several evidence-qualified opportunities without weakening any existing gate.

The objective is **breadth with discipline**:
- more independent opportunities;
- no forced action;
- no market is allowed to borrow a weak signal from another market;
- every lane keeps its own source, leakage, price, sample, and promotion controls.

## Market lanes

### Lane FT — First Touchdown
Use the existing T7/T8/T10, matchup, injury-role, A/B/Dynamic-C and price-hurdle architecture.
This remains a selective high-payoff lane.

### Lane SIDE — Team Spread / Moneyline
Model the game margin using strictly pregame team history and market spread.
Research questions:
- which side is mispriced relative to pregame team form/matchup;
- whether the same signal is better expressed ATS or moneyline;
- whether closing moneyline evidence supports real historical economics.

### Lane TOTAL — Game Over / Under
Model total points using strictly pregame offensive scoring, defensive allowance, pace/opportunity proxies when authenticated, and the posted total.
Research questions:
- whether the game environment is materially above or below market total;
- whether offense/defense style interaction improves total direction;
- whether OVER and UNDER should be treated as separate sublanes.

### Lane PROP — Individual Player Props
Use frozen AlphaProps AP-RB1 research baseline:
- QB passing yards;
- RB rushing yards;
- WR receiving yards/receptions;
- TE receiving yards/receptions.
No betting edge is claimed until an authenticated prop line/price is captured before kickoff.

### Lane TD — Anytime / First-TD Player
Use Player DNA and matchup/role redistribution.
Anytime TD and First TD are separate settlement/price families and must not share profitability claims.

## Weekly opportunity principle
A game can fail First-TD qualification and still qualify in SIDE, TOTAL or PROP.
A player can qualify for a PROP without the game qualifying in FT.
No lane is required to generate a pick.

## Portfolio controls
- Never count correlated tickets as independent opportunities.
- Record market family, game, player, direction, price/line and timestamp.
- Flag same-game correlations.
- Cap worst-case loss at the portfolio layer.
- Do not optimize stake by number of available bets.
- Profitability requires authenticated prices/lines.

## AlphaCreative integration
Agent 24 reviews every AlphaBreadth gate for:
- unexplained misses;
- market translation opportunities;
- overlap/correlation;
- unused data;
- whether a signal belongs in another market.
AlphaCreative may propose new tests but may not bypass lane controls.

## Required weekly output
- FT qualifiers
- SIDE qualifiers
- TOTAL qualifiers
- PROP candidates
- TD candidates
- overlaps/correlation warnings
- PRICE/SOURCE status
- no-action lanes
- total number of independent candidate opportunities
- worst-case portfolio exposure
- prospective/shadow/live-research status

## Promotion
A lane may only be promoted after its own predeclared historical/validation/prospective controls pass.
One successful market does not validate another.

## External action
Research only.
No wager placement or sportsbook transmission.
